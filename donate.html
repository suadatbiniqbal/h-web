Transparency & FAQ

Is donating required to use Harmber?
No! Harmber is completely free and open-source. All features remain unlocked for everyone whether you donate or not.

  • Are payments secure? — Yes. UPI payments happen directly inside your installed UPI banking apps (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, etc.). No card or banking credentials are collected by this site.
  • Other ways to contribute? — If you can't contribute financially, starring the repository on GitHub, sharing Harmber with friends, or reporting bugs helps immensely!
